Recommended actions

Proposed for the week 17 owner meeting

  1. Test a two-bedroom incentive for three weeks

    For example, one month free on a 13-month lease. Review tours and applications weekly and stop early if two-bedroom velocity doesn't improve.

  2. Shift listing spend toward the channels that tour

    Referrals and the building website tour at 31% and 22%. Social media tours at 9%; cut its spend and boost the website.

  3. Add Saturday open houses

    Tours convert well, so more tour slots should mean more leases. Two open houses a month, promoted to all open leads.

  4. Hold a one-hour response time on weekday leads

    With fewer leads coming in, every inquiry matters. Track response time in the weekly report.
