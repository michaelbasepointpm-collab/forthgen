What I found · checked 25 October 2026

Plenty of interest. A few small things cost viewings.

None of this is unusual for a lean team running six buildings. It's exactly the kind of thing a dedicated leasing desk catches. Tap a screenshot to enlarge it.

"Book appointment by messaging back to receive a phone number (don't text)."Every Marketplace ad
Inquiries

About 200 messages per suite, answered one by one

Each reply gets a phone number by hand, then the renter has to call, not text. That's four steps before a viewing is booked, multiplied by every suite, and it lands on your resident managers.

  • "The pictures are partly generated by AI."
