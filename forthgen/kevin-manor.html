About the building

Life at Kevin Manor

C. & L. Glassman built Kevin Manor with 64 one-bedroom suites, each with its own balcony. Today it also has brand-new garden-level homes with private patios, air conditioning and their own entrances.

The Forth Gen office is on the ground floor, a live-in resident manager looks after the building, and English Bay, Davie and Denman are a block or two away.

  • Live-in resident manager
  • New garden homes with private patios
  • Balconies
  • Dishwashers and hardwood floors
  • Laundry room
  • Forth Gen office on site

Every home includes heat, hot water, a storage locker and bike storage. One-year leases, unfurnished, no pets or smoking.
