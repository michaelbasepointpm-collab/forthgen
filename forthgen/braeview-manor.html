About the building

Life at Braeview Manor

Braeview Manor looks onto Alexandra Park and its old bandstand, steps from the English Bay bathhouse and the seawall. Its one- and two-bedroom homes have big windows and balconies, and many look out over the ocean, the mountains and the city.

The top floor holds two-bedroom penthouses. A live-in resident manager looks after the building, and Denman and Davie are around the corner.

  • Live-in resident manager
  • Ocean, mountain and city views
  • Balconies
  • Dishwashers and hardwood floors
  • Laundry room (in-suite in some homes)
  • Secured underground parking (extra)

Every home includes heat, hot water, a storage locker and bike storage. One-year leases, unfurnished, no pets or smoking.
