About the building

Life at Bayside Towers

Bayside Towers stands in the part of the West End locals call "west of Denman": leafy, quiet and close to everything. Its one-bedroom homes sit above mature gardens, with an elevator, a laundry room, bike storage and lockers.

Denman Street’s groceries, cafés and restaurants are a block away. Stanley Park and English Bay are a short walk, and the 5 and 23 buses stop nearby.

  • Elevator
  • Laundry room
  • Bike storage
  • Storage lockers
  • Parking (extra)
  • Heat and hot water included

Every home includes heat, hot water, a storage locker and bike storage. One-year leases, unfurnished, no pets or smoking.
