Our story

Family-built. Family-run.

Forth Gen Holdings looks after six rental buildings in Vancouver's West End. Some of them were built by the same family in the 1960s, and sold with hand-painted signs that still exist today.

Four generations

The same streets, the same care

In the 1960s, C. & L. Glassman built apartment towers a block from English Bay. The signs promised a balcony for each apartment, cablevision, coloured plumbing and "every modern convenience". Elliot Towers offered "all corner suites, all view suites".

In the late 1970s, Glassman Holdings added Rosewoods on Pendrell Street, "a quality building" with studios, one- and two-bedroom suites. Today the family's fourth generation runs Forth Gen, looking after 378 homes in six buildings.

  1. 1962–63

    C. & L. Glassman build Kevin Manor and Elliot Towers in the West End.

  2. 1979

    Glassman Holdings builds Rosewoods, a four-storey garden building on Pendrell Street.

  3. Today

    Forth Gen Holdings, the fourth generation, runs six buildings: Bayside Towers, Braeview Manor, Elliot Towers, Heather House, Kevin Manor and Rosewoods.

  4. Every turnover

    Homes are renovated as they open up: hardwood floors, stone counters, new kitchens and bathrooms.
