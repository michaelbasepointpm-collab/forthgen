About the building

Life at Heather House

Heather House is a concrete high-rise one block from English Bay. Every home is a one-bedroom, with more storage than most and plenty of windows; on the upper floors, many look out over the ocean, the mountains and the city.

A live-in resident manager runs the building. Groceries, cafés and the seawall are all within a few minutes’ walk.

  • Live-in resident manager
  • Panoramic views from upper floors
  • Balconies
  • Dishwashers and hardwood floors
  • Extra storage
  • Secured underground parking (extra)

Every home includes heat, hot water, a storage locker and bike storage. One-year leases, unfurnished, no pets or smoking.
