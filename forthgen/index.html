Our story

Built by one family. Still run by it.

In the 1960s, C. & L. Glassman built West End towers like Kevin Manor and Elliot Towers, and sold them with hand-painted signs. Forth Gen is the family's fourth generation, still looking after the same streets.

"Kevin Manor. 64 luxury 1-bedroom suites. Balcony for each apartment."

Renting with us

Three simple steps

  1. Find a home

    Browse what's available now, or join the waitlist for the building and size you want. We contact the waitlist first.

  2. Book a viewing

    Choose a day and time online. Every viewing is in person, with Michael or the building's resident manager.

  3. Apply

    After your viewing, apply with references and a damage deposit. Leases are one year, unfurnished, with heat and hot water included.

Questions? Ask Michael.

Michael looks after leasing for all six buildings. Message him any time, or book a viewing and he’ll confirm by text or email.
