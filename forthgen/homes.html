Renting with us

Questions people ask

What’s included in the rent?

Heat, hot water, a storage locker and bike storage. Parking is available in each building at extra cost.

How long is the lease?

One year. After that it carries on month to month under the Residential Tenancy Act.

Are pets allowed?

Sorry, no pets. Our buildings are also non-smoking, and BBQs aren’t allowed on balconies.

Are the homes furnished?

No, all homes are unfurnished.

Where do I do laundry?

Every building has a laundry room. Some homes have their own washer and dryer; the listing says so when they do.

How do viewings work?

Book online, by message or by text. Every viewing is in person, so you see the actual home before you apply.

What do I need to apply?

A completed application, employment and landlord references, a credit check and a damage deposit of half a month’s rent.

None of the homes fit. What now?

Join the waitlist with the buildings and size you want. When a matching home opens up, you hear about it before it’s advertised.

Didn’t see the right home?

Tell Michael what you’re looking for. New homes open up every month, and the waitlist hears first.
