About the building

Life at Rosewoods

Glassman Holdings built Rosewoods in the late 1970s on a leafy block of Pendrell Street. Its homes are among the roomiest in the West End: studios, one-bedrooms, one-bedrooms with a den, top-floor lofts and two-bedrooms, most with large balconies over the gardens.

Denman and Davie are a block away, English Bay two, and Stanley Park a short walk.

  • Large balconies
  • Top-floor lofts
  • Dens and extra storage
  • Dishwashers and hardwood floors
  • Laundry room, bike storage and lockers
  • Secured underground parking (extra)

Every home includes heat, hot water, a storage locker and bike storage. One-year leases, unfurnished, no pets or smoking.
